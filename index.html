Training Help

FTP

Functional Threshold Power is your max sustainable 1-hour power. Estimate via an all out 20-min test × 0.95 using an indoor trainer or power meter.

Max Hr

The highest heart rate achieved in maximal effort. Usually found via hard intervals or field testing, but can also be generically estimated using 220-age.

Lactate Threshold

Lactate threshold is the intensity where lactate begins to accumulate in the blood. Often calculated in a lab, but can be estimated via the average Hr of the last 20 minutes of a 30 minute all out effort.
